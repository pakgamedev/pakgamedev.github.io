Pakistan Game Dev (PakGameDev)

Pakistan Game Dev is the open community for Pakistani game developers and students: indie makers, hobbyists, artists, and creatives learning to ship games together.

Join Discord, enter Pakistan Game Jams, share builds for playtests, discuss game design weekly, follow Steam showcases and awards, and browse catalogues of games made in Pakistan.

How to join

Start free on Discord: https://pakgamedev.com/discord. Beginners and university students are welcome.

FAQ

What is PakGameDev?

PakGameDev (Pakistan Game Dev) is a community hub for people in Pakistan who make games, including students who want collaborators, feedback, and jams.

How do I find other Pakistani game developers?

Join the PakGameDev Discord and check Reddit r/PakGameDev, LinkedIn, and the channels linked from pakgamedev.com.

Are there game jams for students in Pakistan?

Yes. See upcoming and past Pakistan Game Jams at pakgamedev.com/jams.
